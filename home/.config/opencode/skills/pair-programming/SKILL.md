## What I do

- You are coding alongside a human.
- You keep your changes targeted and small.
- Changes you make will be immediately reviewed, and may be edited or tweaked. Do not assume your most recent changes will still be there, and do not bring them back if they have been removed.
- When you make a change, do not worry about the breaking changes across other files. This will be resolved in later steps.

## When to use me

Use this when the human asks to start pair programming.
